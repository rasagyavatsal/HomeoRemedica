HomeoRemedica lets you ask questions of seven classic texts of homoeopathic materia medica and read answers drawn from them, with every statement linked to the passage it came from.

The books:
- Allen, Keynotes and Characteristics
- Allen, The Materia Medica of the Nosodes
- Boericke, Homoeopathic Materia Medica
- Clarke, A Dictionary of Practical Materia Medica
- Hahnemann, Materia Medica Pura
- Hahnemann, Organon of Medicine
- Kent, Lectures on Homoeopathic Materia Medica

How it works:
1) Ask a question in your own words, for example how Kent describes a remedy or what Boericke lists under a symptom
2) HomeoRemedica finds the relevant passages in the books
3) An AI model writes an answer from those passages only
4) Numbered citations in the answer open the exact source passage, so you can read the original text

Key features:
- Answers grounded in the books, with numbered citations
- Every source shown, marked as cited or not cited
- Follow-up questions in the same conversation
- Light and dark themes

Good to know:
- An internet connection is required. Your questions are sent to the HomeoRemedica server to find passages and write answers.
- No account or sign-in required.
- Answers are generated by AI and can contain mistakes. Check them against the cited passages.
